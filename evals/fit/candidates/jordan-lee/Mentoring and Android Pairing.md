# Mentoring and Android Pairing

> Fictional eval note for fitcheck.

> Summary
> "I mentored two junior iOS engineers through their first App Store releases. I also paired with our Android team for a few weeks to port the offline sync design to Kotlin, though the Android app itself was theirs."

## What I did

- Ran weekly code reviews and pairing sessions with two junior iOS engineers.
- Wrote our iOS onboarding guide and release checklist.
- Paired with the Android team to explain the sync design while they wrote it in Kotlin. I read and reviewed Kotlin but did not ship an Android app.

## Result

- Both junior engineers ran a release on their own within six months.
